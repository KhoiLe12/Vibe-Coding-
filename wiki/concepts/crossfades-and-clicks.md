# Crossfades and clicks

A cut at a non-zero sample produces a click. Mitigate with zero-crossing snapping plus a short (5–10 ms) equal-power crossfade.

Used by [[chop]] and by seamless loops in [[playback-and-scrub]] and [[loop-pedal]].
